# SYNTHETIC Atrial Fibrillation Anticoagulation Guideline (version 2.0)

## 1. Scope
This guideline covers adults with non-valvular atrial fibrillation.

## 2. Stroke prevention
Offer apixaban 5 mg twice daily to adults with non-valvular atrial fibrillation and a CHA2DS2-VASc score of 1 or more. (Evidence level A)
Reduce apixaban to 2.5 mg twice daily if the patient meets at least two of: age 80 years or older, weight 60 kg or less, serum creatinine 133 micromol/L or above. (Evidence level B)
Apixaban is contraindicated in patients with a mechanical heart valve. (Evidence level A)

## 3. Antiplatelet therapy
The previous recommendation to use aspirin for stroke prevention in atrial fibrillation has been withdrawn and aspirin is no longer recommended for this indication. (Evidence level A)
