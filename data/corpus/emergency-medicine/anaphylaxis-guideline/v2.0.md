# SYNTHETIC Anaphylaxis Emergency Treatment Guideline (version 2.0)

## 1. Scope
This guideline covers adults with anaphylaxis in the emergency department.

## 2. Adrenaline
Give intramuscular adrenaline 0.5 mg into the anterolateral thigh to adults with anaphylaxis. (Evidence level A)
Repeat intramuscular adrenaline after 5 minutes if there is no improvement. (Evidence level A)
Intravenous adrenaline must not be given as a bolus outside critical care settings. (Evidence level B)

## 3. Adjunct therapy
The previous recommendation to give chlorphenamine as first-line treatment for anaphylaxis has been withdrawn. (Evidence level A)
