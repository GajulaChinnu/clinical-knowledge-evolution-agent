# SYNTHETIC Anaphylaxis Emergency Treatment Guideline (version 1.0)

## 1. Scope
This guideline covers adults with anaphylaxis in the emergency department.

## 2. Adrenaline
Give intramuscular adrenaline 0.5 mg into the anterolateral thigh to adults with anaphylaxis. (Evidence level A)
Repeat intramuscular adrenaline after 10 minutes if there is no improvement. (Evidence level B)

## 3. Adjunct therapy
Give chlorphenamine 10 mg intravenously as first-line treatment for anaphylaxis. (Evidence level C)
