# SYNTHETIC Safety Notice: Dabigatran and Severe Renal Impairment

## 1. Advice for prescribers
Dabigatran is contraindicated in patients with eGFR below 30 mL/min/1.73m2. (Evidence level A)

## 2. Reason
Dabigatran is renally cleared and accumulates in severe renal impairment, increasing bleeding risk. (Evidence level A)
