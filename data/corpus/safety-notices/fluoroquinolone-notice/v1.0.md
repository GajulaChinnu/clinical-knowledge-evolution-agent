# SYNTHETIC Safety Notice: Fluoroquinolones in Community-Acquired Pneumonia

## 1. Advice for prescribers
Fluoroquinolones such as levofloxacin and ciprofloxacin must not be used for low-severity community-acquired pneumonia when alternatives are available. (Evidence level B)

## 2. Reason
Fluoroquinolones carry a risk of tendon rupture and aortic aneurysm. (Evidence level B)
