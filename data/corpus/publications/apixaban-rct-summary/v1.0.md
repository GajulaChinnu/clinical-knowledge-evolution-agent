# SYNTHETIC Trial Summary: Apixaban versus Aspirin in Atrial Fibrillation

## 1. Design
A randomised controlled trial enrolled 4200 adults with atrial fibrillation. (Evidence level A)

## 2. Findings
Apixaban 5 mg twice daily reduced stroke compared with aspirin 75 mg once daily without increasing major bleeding. (Evidence level A)
