# SYNTHETIC Community-Acquired Pneumonia Antibiotic Guideline (version 2.0)

## 1. Scope
This guideline covers adults with community-acquired pneumonia.

## 2. Low-severity pneumonia
Give amoxicillin 500 mg three times daily for 5 days to adults with low-severity community-acquired pneumonia. (Evidence level A)
Give clarithromycin 500 mg twice daily for 5 days if the patient has a penicillin allergy. (Evidence level B)
Avoid clarithromycin in patients taking simvastatin because of the risk of myopathy. (Evidence level B)

## 3. Alternative therapy
The previous recommendation to use doxycycline as an alternative has been withdrawn. (Evidence level B)
