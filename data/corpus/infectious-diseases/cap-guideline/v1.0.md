# SYNTHETIC Community-Acquired Pneumonia Antibiotic Guideline (version 1.0)

## 1. Scope
This guideline covers adults with community-acquired pneumonia.

## 2. Low-severity pneumonia
Give amoxicillin 500 mg three times daily for 7 days to adults with low-severity community-acquired pneumonia. (Evidence level A)
Give clarithromycin 500 mg twice daily for 7 days if the patient has a penicillin allergy. (Evidence level B)

## 3. Alternative therapy
Doxycycline 200 mg on the first day then 100 mg once daily may be used as an alternative for low-severity community-acquired pneumonia. (Evidence level C)
