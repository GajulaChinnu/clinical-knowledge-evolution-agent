# SYNTHETIC Chronic Kidney Disease Management Guideline (version 1.0)

## 1. Scope
This guideline covers adults with chronic kidney disease.

## 2. Albuminuria
Start ramipril 2.5 mg once daily in adults with chronic kidney disease and an albumin-to-creatinine ratio of 30 mg/mmol or above. (Evidence level A)
Check serum potassium within 2 weeks of starting ramipril. (Evidence level B)

## 3. Metabolic acidosis
Offer sodium bicarbonate 500 mg three times daily when serum bicarbonate is below 20 mmol/L. (Evidence level C)
