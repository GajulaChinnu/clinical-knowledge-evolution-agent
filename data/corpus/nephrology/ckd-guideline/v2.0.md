# SYNTHETIC Chronic Kidney Disease Management Guideline (version 2.0)

## 1. Scope
This guideline covers adults with chronic kidney disease.

## 2. Albuminuria
Start ramipril 2.5 mg once daily in adults with chronic kidney disease and an albumin-to-creatinine ratio of 3 mg/mmol or above. (Evidence level A)
Check serum potassium within 2 weeks of starting ramipril. (Evidence level B)
Ramipril must not be combined with an angiotensin receptor blocker such as losartan. (Evidence level A)

## 3. Metabolic acidosis
The previous recommendation for routine sodium bicarbonate supplementation has been withdrawn pending further evidence. (Evidence level B)
