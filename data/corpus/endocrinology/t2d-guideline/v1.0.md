# SYNTHETIC Type 2 Diabetes Pharmacotherapy Guideline (version 1.0)

## 1. Scope
This guideline covers adults with type 2 diabetes managed in secondary care.

## 2. First-line therapy
Start metformin 500 mg once daily with the evening meal in adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes. (Evidence level A)
Increase metformin by 500 mg every 2 weeks to a maximum of 2000 mg daily if tolerated. (Evidence level B)

## 3. Renal dosing of metformin
Metformin may be continued when eGFR is 30 mL/min/1.73m2 or above. (Evidence level B)

## 4. Second-line therapy
Gliclazide 40 mg once daily may be added as second-line therapy when HbA1c remains above target. (Evidence level C)
Consider empagliflozin 10 mg once daily in adults with established cardiovascular disease. (Evidence level A)
