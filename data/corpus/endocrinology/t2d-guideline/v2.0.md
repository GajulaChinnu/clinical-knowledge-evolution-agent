# SYNTHETIC Type 2 Diabetes Pharmacotherapy Guideline (version 2.0)

## 1. Scope
This guideline covers adults with type 2 diabetes managed in secondary care.

## 2. First-line therapy
Start metformin 500 mg twice daily with meals in adults with newly diagnosed type 2 diabetes. (Evidence level A)
Increase metformin by 500 mg every 2 weeks to a maximum of 2000 mg daily if tolerated. (Evidence level B)

## 3. Renal dosing of metformin
Metformin may be continued when eGFR is 45 mL/min/1.73m2 or above. (Evidence level A)
Reduce metformin to a maximum of 1000 mg daily when eGFR is 30 to 44 mL/min/1.73m2. (Evidence level B)
Metformin is contraindicated when eGFR is below 30 mL/min/1.73m2. (Evidence level A)
Metformin is contraindicated within 48 hours after iodinated contrast in patients with eGFR below 60 mL/min/1.73m2. (Evidence level B)

## 4. Second-line therapy
Consider empagliflozin 10 mg once daily in adults with established cardiovascular disease. (Evidence level A)
The previous recommendation to add gliclazide as routine second-line therapy has been withdrawn. (Evidence level B)
